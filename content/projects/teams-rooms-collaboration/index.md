Meeting room technology needs to be reliable for people who simply want to walk into a room and start a meeting.

## Scope

- Supporting Microsoft Teams meeting-room workflows
- Assisting with room calendar and resource mailbox behaviour
- Troubleshooting meeting access, invitations and room displays
- Contributing to suitable room hardware planning

## Practical Focus

My work in this area centres on reducing complexity for users and ensuring the meeting experience is straightforward, particularly when multiple locations or organisers are involved.

## Key Takeaway

Reliable meeting spaces depend on both correct configuration and clear meeting practices. Technology works best when the user process is simple.
